# Audio application icons

Official 192×192 PNG icons, vendored unchanged for offline launcher/media-list
rendering. Brand artwork and trademarks belong to their respective owners; these
copies identify the corresponding applications and imply no affiliation.

- `audible.png`: the 192px favicon linked by https://www.audible.co.uk/
  - Source: https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/seo/creative/Audible_Google_Favicon_192px.png
  - SHA-256: `8d459fb8035a99f7104df1f40c50d30f66e0622e1969f020b5e81a0d3f97cf44`
- `pocketcasts.png`: Pocket Casts' official Android launcher icon
  - Source: https://github.com/Automattic/pocket-casts-android/blob/626e5c278af37a88b5bf6e3e1d357406c4d34cbf/modules/services/images/src/main/res/mipmap-xxxhdpi/ic_launcher.png
  - SHA-256: `f95b60c9d198881c2b4e1799cf92b892ce691858d1abdb3454dfd6d2f637226e`

Home Manager installs these in the user's hicolor icon theme, named after their
`com.laufan.*` desktop IDs. Chrome profile paths and logins are unaffected.
